Country & tax settings

Default allowance is €1,000 for an individual. Jointly assessed spouses generally have €2,000. Enter only the portion not already represented in this app. Starting carryforwards apply to the current calendar year.

Listed equity: STCG 20% (held ≤ 12 months), LTCG 12.5% on gains above the annual exemption (held > 12 months). Cess is 4% on the tax. You choose STCG or LTCG when adding a trade. Estimates only.
