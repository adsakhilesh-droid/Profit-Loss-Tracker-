Country & tax settings

Profit color applies to charts and all positive (green) amounts. Loss is always red.

Always red

Default allowance is €1,000 for an individual. Jointly assessed spouses generally have €2,000. Enter only the portion not already represented in this app. Starting carryforwards apply to the current calendar year.

Listed equity: STCG 20% (held ≤ 12 months), LTCG 12.5% on gains above the annual exemption (held > 12 months). Cess is 4% on the tax. You choose STCG or LTCG when adding a trade. Estimates only.

US (2025): ST gains at your marginal rate; LT gains 0% up to the bracket, then 15%/20%. Netting: ST↔ST, LT↔LT, then net ST offsets net LT. Wash-sale rule and the $3,000/yr offset against ordinary income are not modeled.

UK (2024/25+): gains above the AEA (£3,000) taxed at 18%/24% on shares. Losses offset gains and carry forward; the AEA cannot be carried over.

France: securities gains are taxed at the flat PFU of 30% (12.8% income tax + 17.2% social contributions). The progressive-scale opt-in, holding-period allowances (50%/65%) and the PEA wrapper are not modeled.

Japan: listed-share gains taxed separately at 20.315% (15% national + 5% local + 0.315% reconstruction surtax). No exemption, no offset against salary. Losses carry forward up to 3 years if you file a final return.

China: gains on listed A-shares are currently exempt from individual income tax, so the estimate is 0%. Dividends are 20% with relief for holdings over 1 year (not modeled).
